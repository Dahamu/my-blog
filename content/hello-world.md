This post is plain Markdown. Drop a new `.md` file in `content/` and it shows up on the home page.

## What you can write

- Lists, **bold**, *italic*, and [links](https://nextjs.org)
- Tables, task lists, and strikethrough (via GitHub-flavoured Markdown)

> Good typography is mostly about getting out of the reader's way.
